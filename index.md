## 👋 Hello!

I'm **Vivek Raj**, a PhD candidate in Biomedical Engineering at **IIT Delhi** (defense: July 2026), working at the intersection of **computer vision, deep learning, and assistive robotics**.

For five years I've built and clinically validated **Optical Myography (OMG)** — a camera-based alternative to EMG that lets a standard USB camera decode proportional hand and wrist intent for prosthetic control, without electrodes or skin contact. The system has been tested end-to-end, from lab bench to **clinical trials with transradial amputees at AIIMS New Delhi**, achieving up to 100% task success with real-time (<90 ms) inference.

- Real-time **computer vision** pipelines (Kalman filtering, marker & markerless tracking)
- **Deep learning** segmentation (PyTorch, SAM2, MobileNetV3) for fully markerless control
- Multimodal **sensor fusion** (camera + IMU + data glove)
- **Clinical human-subject research** on amputee and able-bodied cohorts
